Why EchoType

Built for the AI-coding loop

You prompt Claude Code or Cursor dozens of times a day, and typing is the slowest link. Hold a hotkey, say what you want, release — punctuated text appears at your cursor in about a second, in whatever app has focus.

Whisper mode is not a metaphor

The audio pipeline is tuned for quiet and whispered speech — gain-boosted, filtered, and guarded against hallucination — so late-night sessions and open offices don't mean broadcasting your prompts. A dedicated quiet mode switches to our most accurate model with one click.

Your voice never leaves the machine

Transcription runs entirely on-device on Apple Silicon. No account, no cloud, no audio retention — dictate proprietary code context without a security review. It works the same on a plane with Wi-Fi off.

Speaks fluent developer

useEffect, kubectl, your project's names — EchoType ships tuned for technical vocabulary and learns yours: add custom terms once, stop fixing them forever.

Questions

Is my audio really never uploaded?

Correct. Capture, processing, and transcription all happen on your machine — there is no account and no server in the dictation path. The app works fully offline.

What do I need to run it?

An Apple Silicon Mac. Windows and Linux are on the roadmap — the entire stack is cross-platform by design. Join the beta list below if you want either.

Does it work in the terminal?

Terminals are the primary target — verified in macOS Terminal, VS Code (editor and integrated terminal), and Chrome, with a clipboard fallback for apps that reject synthetic keystrokes.

How is this different from macOS built-in dictation?

Built-in dictation wants clear, loud speech, stumbles on technical terms, and isn't built for the prompt-writing loop. EchoType is tuned for quiet speech and developer vocabulary, injects anywhere, and never phones home.

Voice commands? Meeting notes?

No — EchoType does one thing: turn your speech into text at your cursor. For voice control see Talon; for meetings see Granola.

What does the beta cost?

Nothing. EchoType is free while in beta. A paid Pro tier (larger models, streaming, per-app profiles) arrives at v1.0 — beta users get an early-bird deal.
